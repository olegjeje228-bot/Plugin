FILE: FpvDrone/DroneInstance.cs
========================================
Purpose: Core drone logic - physics, piloting, camera, HUD, combat

Key classes/fields:
- DroneInstance (sealed class)
  - Id, Owner, Pilot, Dummy, Schematic, SoundPlayer
  - Position, Rotation, Velocity (Vector3 physics)
  - BatteryVoltage (7.01V start), Hp (400), Ammo (5), IsActive/Piloting/Destroyed/Dropping
  - OriginalRole/Position/Health/Inventory/Ammo (state save/restore)
  - _selfDestructPresses, _confirmingDestruct (3-tap R)
  - Physics constants: MaxHSpeed=33.3, Accel=15, Elasticity=0.35, G=9.81, Range=400

Key methods:
- StartPhysics() - begins physics + sound coroutines
- StartPiloting(Player) - saves state, spawns dummy, enters camera, starts HUD/Cam loops
- StopPiloting(bool restore) - kills coroutines, restores state, removes dummy
- Destroy(bool explode) - kills everything, optional HE explosion
- TakeDamage(float) - subtracts HP, destroys at 0
- OnKey(id, pressed) - handles exit/drop/destruct keybinds
- PhysicsLoop() - 0.05s tick: battery drain, input, movement, collision, ground, tesla
- UpdateBat(float dt) - complex drain model based on movement/ammo
- GetInput() - WASD+QE normalized vector
- WallHit() - BoxCast collision with damage reflection
- GroundCheck() - Raycast 0.15m
- TeslaCheck() - GameObject.FindGameObjectsWithTag("TeslaGate")
- DoorHit() - opens unsecured doors within 2m
- HitPlayers() - ram damage to players
- DropSeq() - drops all grenade payload
- HudLoop() - broadcasts speed/battery/ammo every 0.2s
- CamLoop() - sets pilot position/rotation at camera offset every 0.05s
- SoundLoop() / MakeSound() - AudioPlayerApi via reflection
- EnterCamera() - sets Tutorial, scale 0.0193, Invisible, NightVision, FogControl
- RestoreState() - restores role, position, inventory, ammo
- SpawnDummy() - Npc.Spawn with original items + radio
- SyncSchem() / DestroySchem() / PlayAnim() - ProjectMER reflection
